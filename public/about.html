A clearer view of Kochi.

Kochi Traffic is a public, non-commercial project covering the wider Kochi urban area, including Kakkanad, Kalamassery, Aluva, and Tripunithura.

Traffic and journey estimates

Maps, traffic observations, address searches, and routes come from TomTom. The traffic layer refreshes every 15 minutes while the page is visible. A fetch time records when this app acquired data, rather than when every vehicle was observed. Missing data does not mean an empty road.

Current speed is an average for an observed road segment. Free-flow speed describes expected uncongested conditions and is not the legal speed limit. Journey times for leaving now or in one hour are provider estimates and can change. Two-wheeler routing uses TomTom's experimental motorcycle mode; restriction coverage can be incomplete. Follow road signs and local rules.

Your data

There are no user accounts, ads, or saved trip histories. Address searches and selected trip coordinates are sent through our Cloudflare server to TomTom to answer your request. Map requests identify the area being viewed. Device location is optional, requested only after you choose “Use my location,” and can be denied in your browser.

The application database stores aggregate request counts and short-lived rate-limit counters. Visitor counters use a secret-salted identifier that changes daily; raw IP addresses, addresses, and journey coordinates are not stored in that database. Short-lived identifiers are removed by hourly cleanup. Aggregate counts are retained for up to 33 UTC dates. Cloudflare and TomTom process requests under their own privacy policies.

Free public access

Shared usage limits keep the project within free allowances. If a limit is reached or accounting is unavailable, requests stop instead of bypassing the limits. Daily application limits reset at 5:30 am IST. Other limits can take longer to clear.
